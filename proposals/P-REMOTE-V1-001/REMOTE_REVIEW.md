Reviewer: PM, direct design/source desk review, 2026-10-11. Distinct REMOTE assessment, not a separate device-session signoff or hardware proof. Exact artifact: CCM_EXACT_DIFF.patch / DIFF_MANIFEST.json.

Verdict: **CONDITIONALLY IMPLEMENTABLE design direction; NOT QUALIFIED / NOT IMPLEMENTED**. Removing the coherent whole-domain adversary removes the information-theoretic external monotonic-authority dependency. It does not make the existing A005 Candidate pass G1a or authorize its laboratory mode in production.

Candidate design must serialize a single writer; append complete typed records with schema/type/length/checksum/hash and generation/request/effect/allocator links; validate expected generation; publish a generation/root commit only after referenced records are durably ordered and validated. Retain prior/new roots and records until recovery/compaction is safe. Actual NVS setter, readback and no-op nvs_commit are not a compound transaction or persistence proof. Explicit commit-point/acknowledgement semantics and physical qualification are required.

Recovery scans/validates all reachable records and root relationships, distinguishes incomplete prepared data from committed history only with justified commit evidence, and never chooses the highest CRC-valid generation merely because it exists. An old valid root with orphan/new records or uncertain completed commit blocks affected scopes when newest committed state cannot be established. It must not silently resurrect allocator IDs, erase barriers or rerun commands. Conservative blocking after benign interrupted preparation is permissible until exact recovery rules prove a safe choice.

Request/effect/barrier correlation, accepted-history allocator non-reuse, immutable evidence, proof growth and fencing stay mandatory. Recovery emits zero commands; an independent witness records actual emission/admission outside the faulted domain. Test ordinary power-loss before/after every append/chunk/index/root/GC step, ambiguous API outcomes with/without persistence, stale root/record combinations, corruption, concurrent/reentrant writer, counters, full capacity and interrupted/unknown-schema migration. Demonstrate no replay after an acknowledged emission and preserve barriers when acknowledgement is ambiguous. Whole-domain restore tests must say OUTSIDE, never PASS.

Capacity remains BLOCKED pending enforced bounds and measured physical entry/fragmentation/GC/latency/endurance evidence. No durable Core backend exists; no production wiring/firmware/partition change or device acceptance in this review. Frozen CCM1.0.0 remains effective until separately approved replacement.
